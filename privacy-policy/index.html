Privacy policy

How we collect, use and look after your personal information.

Laundrowash (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and our shop, how we use it, and how you can contact us about it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

We only collect the information we need to provide our services. This may include:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • your pickup or delivery address and suburb;
  • details about the laundry services you’ve asked for; and
  • anything else you choose to tell us when you contact us.

Like most websites, our site and the services it uses (such as the embedded Google Map) may automatically record basic technical information, such as your browser type and IP address.

How we use it

  • to respond to your enquiry or quote request;
  • to arrange pickups, deliveries and collections;
  • to provide and invoice our services; and
  • to let you know about changes that affect your order.

We will not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share it where needed to deliver our services (for example, passing garment details to our dry cleaning partner) or where required by law.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. We keep it only for as long as we need it.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you at any time. Just contact us using the details below.

Third-party websites

Our website links to other sites, such as Facebook and Google Maps. We’re not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.
