Why use us?

Why not just wash it at home?

Often it simply isn’t practical. Household machines aren’t built for heavily soiled work garments, and grease and grime can end up on the rest of the family’s washing.

  • Modern, heavy-duty industrial washers and dryers made for the toughest cleaning jobs
  • Keeps grime out of your employees’ homes and off their family’s laundry
  • Well-kept workwear lasts longer and keeps your crew looking professional
  • A regular, reliable schedule built around your shifts
