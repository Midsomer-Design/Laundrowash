What we do

Everything the household wash needs

Bring in as much or as little as you like. A single shirt, a week’s washing or the whole linen cupboard.

Wash, dry & fold

Drop off your washing and collect it clean, dry and neatly folded, ready to go straight in the drawers. Weekly service available.

Ironing & folding

Ironing baskets from $30, with individual items welcome. Shirts, school uniforms and work clothes pressed and returned on hangers.

Doonas, quilts & bedding

Doonas, quilts, blankets, underlays and sheets. Even pillows can be washed in our big, efficient machines. From $15.

Mending & alterations

Dropped hems, split seams and missing buttons fixed while your washing’s being done. Ask us for a quote.

Dry cleaning

We’re agents for Moderne Dry Cleaners in Corrimal. Drop your suits, dresses and delicates in with your washing and collect everything together.

Pickup & delivery

Can’t get to us? We can collect from your door and drop it back fresh and folded (conditions apply). Call to check your area.
