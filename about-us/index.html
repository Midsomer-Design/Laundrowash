Local, family-owned and proudly Illawarra.

For more than 25 years, Laundrowash has looked after the washing, ironing and dry cleaning of Wollongong families and businesses from our shop on Waples Road, Unanderra.

  • Hundreds of happy locals

Meet the owner

Keith Somerville

Keith Somerville took over Laundrowash in 2019 with a simple goal: keep the friendly, reliable service our regulars love, and make it even better.

Keith is no stranger to local business. He also runs KeiraPC, a well-known Illawarra IT company with a reputation for quality, service and excellence in home and business computing. He brings those same values to every load that comes through our doors.

Under Keith, the focus is on what matters most: modern, efficient machines, a quality finish on every item, and a friendly face at the counter. He’s also keen to welcome even more locals through the door, whether that’s a family dropping off a single doona or a business that needs its towels turned around every day.

Photo: a customer walking in to the shop carrying a doona and a basket of washing

Walk-ins welcome

Not just a commercial laundry

Plenty of people drive past on busy Waples Road and assume we only do commercial work. Not so!

We love having new customers walk in with their clothes, bedding, quilts, sheets and blankets. Even pillows can be washed in our efficient, modern machines. Bring your laundry in and we’ll take care of it, with a warm welcome every time.
