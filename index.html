Questions? We’ve got answers

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. We offer free pickup and delivery for local business and industrial customers. Give us a call on (02) 4272 4433 to check your suburb and book a time.

How quickly will I get my laundry back?

Most orders are ready quickly, and same-day service is often available if you drop off early in the day. Let us know when you need it and we’ll do our best to make it happen.

Do you wash doonas, quilts and blankets?

We do, and pillows too! Doona and blanket washing starts from $15 for a single. See our full bedding prices.

Do you do dry cleaning?

Yes. We’re agents for Moderne Dry Cleaners, so you can drop off your dry cleaning with your washing and ironing and collect it all from one place.

How much does ironing cost?

Ironing baskets start from $30, and individual items are welcome. Check our pricing page for details.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes, we look after dozens of local businesses, including hairdressers, beauty and massage clinics, dentists, cafés, restaurants, schools, mechanics and heavy industry. We’ll set up a regular pickup schedule and a price to suit. Learn more about business laundry.

Do you sell gift certificates?

We do! Gift certificates make a thoughtful present for new parents, busy friends or anyone who hates ironing. Ask in store about our loyalty cards, too.

Ready to hand over the laundry?

Call us or drop into our Unanderra shop. We’ll take it from there.
