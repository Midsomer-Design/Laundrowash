The fine print, kept as simple as possible.

Our services

We take care to clean every item according to its care label and our experience. Please tell us about any stains, damage, delicate fabrics or special instructions when you drop off or book a pickup.

Pricing and payment

  • Prices shown on our website are a guide only and may change without notice.
  • Final prices depend on the size, fabric and condition of each item. We’ll let you know before starting if a job will cost noticeably more than expected.
  • Payment is due on collection or delivery unless we have agreed account terms with you (for example, for regular business customers).

Pickup and delivery

Pickup and delivery for business and industrial customers is available in selected areas and conditions apply. We’ll confirm availability, times and any charges when you book.

Before you drop off

  • Please check and empty all pockets. We’re not responsible for items left in pockets, or for damage they cause.
  • We can’t be responsible for items that aren’t colourfast, have no care label, or have existing wear, weakness or damage.
  • Some stains can’t be removed completely. We’ll always do our best.

Dry cleaning

Dry cleaning is carried out by our partner, Moderne Dry Cleaners. We act as their agent and will pass on any questions or concerns on your behalf.

If something goes wrong

If you’re not happy with any item, please let us know as soon as possible, ideally within 48 hours of collection or delivery, and bring the item back so we can take a look and put it right.

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under that law.

Uncollected items

Please collect your items promptly. We’ll try to contact you about anything left with us, but items not collected within 90 days may be donated to charity or otherwise disposed of.

Gift certificates and loyalty cards

Gift certificates and loyalty cards can be redeemed in store and cannot be exchanged for cash. Please ask in store about any expiry dates.
